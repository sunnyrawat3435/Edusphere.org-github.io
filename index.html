Trust is built in.

Access rules, history and review are part of the foundation.

  • Role-based access. Students, teachers and parents each see only what their role allows.
  • Audit trail. Grades, payments and approvals record who changed what, and when.
  • School boundaries. One school's records never reach another's.
  • Respect rules. Curiosity Hub posts are scanned. Offensive language removes the anonymity.
